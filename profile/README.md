# TYR-X

### Practical AI software for business, logistics & transportation.

We build software that connects business operations, AI agents, and cybersecurity—grounded in nearly 30 years of logistics and transportation experience.

Founded by [Marc Taylor](https://github.com/stationt50) and based in Dubai, UAE, TYR-X turns operational challenges into tools for clearer visibility, better coordination, and less manual work.

## What we’re building

| Project area | Focus | Stage |
|---|---|---|
| **TYR-X Business Platform** | A mobile-first workspace connecting CRM, communications, tasks, marketing, finance, learning, and AI agents. | Working prototype with simulated services |
| **AI Workforce & Engineering** | Agent task management and coding workflows supported by Git, pull requests, testing, and human review. | Active development and integration testing |
| **Cybersecurity & Exposure Intelligence** | Tools and integrations for exposure monitoring, vulnerability prioritization, security alerts, and remediation tracking. | Active development |
| **Logistics & Shipment Visibility** | Multimodal cargo visibility covering milestones, handoffs, delays, and exception management. | Research and requirements |
| **Local AI & Model Evaluation** | Evaluating locally hosted models for coding and operational tasks, with attention to speed and reliability. | Ongoing evaluation |
| **Business Automation** | Connecting customer relationships, communications, scheduling, documents, and finance. | Active development |

## How we build

We start with the operational problem and develop in manageable steps.

Our approach combines industry experience, AI-assisted software engineering, and open-source tools. Clear requirements, testing, documentation, security, and human oversight guide the work.

Projects span working tools, prototypes, and research. Each project’s documentation should make its current capabilities and limitations clear.

## Public and private development

Most commercial development takes place in private repositories. This organization’s public repositories contain selected projects, documentation, and open-source work.

Forked repositories retain their original authorship and licenses. A fork does not imply that TYR-X created the upstream project.
